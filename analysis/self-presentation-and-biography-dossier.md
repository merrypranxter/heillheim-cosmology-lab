# Dossier — Self-presentation and biography

**Type:** ATLAS READING across REC-008, REC-017, REC-027, REC-033, REC-039, REC-051 (+REC-003, REC-015, REC-020, REC-029)
**Evidence:** mixed; REC-027 and REC-008 transcribed

## Pattern noticed

A consistent self-model recurs: the speaker is a genetically-keyed descendant of ancient royal bloodlines (REC-033, REC-039), modified for information reception (genetic memory), identified by a variant phenotype (REC-011), trained since childhood by CIA school protocols (GATE, REC-039), surviving a 1991 multi-agency assassination attempt (REC-027), and now running a documented multi-year test of government intake channels (REC-008) while distributing a civilization-preservation playbook (REC-002).

Documented self-claims of abilities: telekinesis and spontaneous emergence (REC-003), thermal regulation (REC-015, REC-029), rapid weight modulation (REC-020), resurrection/regeneration (REC-010, REC-023), and "butterfly effect abilities" (REC-017).

## Source-to-source connections

- The 1991 attempt (REC-027) is presented as *provoked* evidence-creation — the speaker claims to have maneuvered agencies into documenting impossible simultaneous presence. This is the biography's hinge event.
- GATE (REC-039) supplies the childhood chapter; the intake test (REC-008) the present-day chapter; the Atlas (REC-002) the mission.
- “35 years and 2 months” of being known to agencies (REC-027) implies agency awareness since approximately mid-1991, internally consistent with the attempt date.

## Alternative readings

- The biography may be read as the cosmology's *origin myth for its author* — each recording adds a chapter rather than evidence.
- Some claims are independently checkable (1991 incident records, GATE program history); checkable and uncheckable claims are deliberately not separated in the source.
