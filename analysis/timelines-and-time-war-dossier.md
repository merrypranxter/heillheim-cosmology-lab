# Dossier — Timelines, Time War, Mandela cluster

**Type:** ATLAS READING across REC-044, REC-047, REC-022, REC-048 (+REC-005, REC-006)
**Evidence:** transcript for REC-044 (inline, no timecodes) and REC-047; summaries elsewhere

## Pattern noticed

The cosmology's temporal mechanics have three interlocking parts:

1. **Save-file branching** (REC-044): humanity sterilized its planet once; the solar system was quantum-locked out of linear time; reality now runs as a finite set of 15 timeline branches (save files), each fix-bound to end-state destruction, fed by holographic projection from outside.
2. **Time war** (REC-047): the 15 variants are at war; shadow-government tampering with celestial technology erased timeline separation; anomalous historical craft (airships, advanced aircraft) are cross-timeline incursions crewed by humans.
3. **Mandela effects** (REC-022, REC-048): legitimate Mandela experiences are memory bleed from competing timelines; but some are manufactured edits (brand memory) by US intelligence. Risen Slain / Einherjar motifs attach to final-war memory carriers.

## Source-to-source connections

- REC-044's "15 timelines" and REC-047's "~15 Earth variants" are presumably the same set (Q-016).
- The quantum-lock premise explains *why* simulation theory is true in-framework and why space probes "stress test" the system (REC-044).
- Timeline 14 (REC-040) is cited as the source of sonic-bone biotech — a concrete claimed resource from a lost branch.

## Alternative readings

- The 4/20 framing (REC-044) may be baked-in register: stoner-holiday numerology as deliberate myth-padding.
- “Manufactured” Mandela effects vs. real ones is an unfalsifiable split unless the speaker supplies criteria.

## What would change this interpretation?
