# EXP-001 — Atlas of Unawakened Worlds

**ATLAS EXPANSION** · Proposed by Heillheim Cosmology Lab contributors · **Not** attributed to @hz.heillheim  
**Source inspirations:** REC-002's themes of civilization, belonging, consciousness and transmission of information.

## Premise
Every society tunes its citizens through distributions of fear, trust, scarcity and solidarity. The Atlas is a dynamic collection of maps; observing one can change the terrain being mapped.

## Proposed mechanism: The Belonging Wave
Collective attention forms a field called the **Belonging Wave**. When communities create conditions of persistent precarity, higher modes of the field attenuate.

This is a new creative mechanism introduced by this expansion, not a scientific conclusion and not a documented claim by the originating creator.

## Proposed places and orders
- **The Cartographers:** custodians split over whether maps only foretell or can cause events.
- **The Sargassum Gate:** threshold imagined where incompatible timelines touch open water.
- **The Fourth Choir:** a network studying dormant capacities and social belonging.
- **The Panoptic Canal:** a channel that ferries historical sequences between incompatible outcomes.

## Tension worth developing
If an Atlas predicts its own consequences, can readers distinguish a prophecy from a mechanism that brought the predicted outcome about?
