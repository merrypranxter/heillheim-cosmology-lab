# Contributing to the Celestial Atlas

Welcome, cartographers, readers, researchers and possible collaborators.

## How to contribute
1. **Correction:** Provide an Atlas source ID, the exact timestamp, your proposed correction, and whether you checked audio or only on-screen captions.
2. **Atlas Reading:** Propose a connection across recordings. Cite both source IDs and mark deductions as deductions.
3. **Atlas Expansion:** Submit an original branch under `atlas-expansions/` with a byline, a short premise, and references to the source elements it develops.
4. **Open question:** Record a precise question in the [clarification ledger](analysis/clarification-ledger.md), without supplying a guessed answer.

## Naming and provenance
Use stable IDs: `REC-001`, `REC-002`; `CLAIM-002-01`; `Q-001`; `EXP-001`. Date a source only when its upload or publication date is confirmed. All timecodes must say whether they refer to the original video or an accelerated edit.

## Rights and conduct
This is a public research/art repository. Do not commit full third-party videos, audio files, copied full transcripts, private chat logs, personal data, or unlicensed artwork. Ask before redistributing recordings. Keep criticism of claims distinct from attacks on people. Do not present independent scientific or historical verification unless a source is attached.

Creator collaboration and attribution preferences are still being established. Contributions do not imply approval by @hz.heillheim.

## Quality bar
No unsourced direct quotes. No assigning inferred motives. No diagnosing someone from their speech or mannerisms. No converting alarming rhetoric into actionable instructions. Keep the door open for corrections.
