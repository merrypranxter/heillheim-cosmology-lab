## Verification checklist
- Locate and preserve the original post link and original date.
- Find the creator's referenced **July 24** prior recording.
- Transcribe actual prediction wording before the real-world event.
- Separate “will happen” from “if it happens” and separate forecast from claimed influence.
- Identify observable outcome, timeframe, and disconfirming result.
- Add independently sourced event timeline *after* the prediction record is frozen.

No claim is marked “fulfilled” merely because later reports resemble one element of the scenario.
