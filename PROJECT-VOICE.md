# Project voice and attribution charter

The Atlas is entered as a **living cosmological map**. Write with imaginative commitment, intellectual curiosity and respect for the person who shared the source work.

## Language
- Call the endeavor **the Celestial Atlas**, **Heillheim Cosmology Lab**, or **the Atlas**.
- Say **“The Atlas describes…”** when reporting its own mechanisms; use **“According to recording 002…”** for a sourced statement.
- Distinguish **ATLAS RECORD**, **ATLAS READING**, and **ATLAS EXPANSION** on every substantial entry.
- Do not use dismissive diagnostic labels, ridicule, or speculate on the creator's motives or private health.
- Do not present unverified claims of extraordinary events or forces as independently established facts.
- New Atlas entities need a contributor attribution. They are not automatically statements by @hz.heillheim.
- A seeming contradiction is a map question until the relevant recordings, dates and phrasing are checked.

## Collaboration
The project steward has asked for permission and invited the originating creator to participate; approval and involvement have **not yet been confirmed here**. Make it easy for him to propose changes, establish preferred terminology, and indicate how he wants to be credited.

## Boundaries
Source records can describe conflict rhetoric and harmful proposals without endorsing or operationalizing them. Do not collect private Discord discussions, organize harassment, or use the archive to recruit people into real-world confrontation.

## One-sentence editorial test
**Could the originating creator recognize his actual words, could a researcher verify where we got them, and could an artist tell what we added?** If yes, the map is doing its job.
