## Source description

This text examines metacognition as a tool to resist social engineering and the erosion of historical truth. It presents a practical, two-step method: first, cease reacting to information, and second, dissociate the immediate response to inspect its source and motives. The author argues that opinions are often engineered rather than grounded in expertise or data, and that understanding the agenda behind information can reveal the web of influence shaping beliefs. The piece also critiques societal power structures in the United States and urges demi humans to detach from ego to reach higher states of consciousness.
