# Cosmology — provisional map

**ATLAS READING • Expanded pass from BATCH-2026-10-08 (REC-002…REC-056)**

The Atlas in the current corpus spans several nested realms of explanation. This is a *research map*, not a final table of laws adopted by the originating creator.

## The six strata

1. **Events:** military movements and possible conflict in named geographic locations (Sargasso Sea, Panama Canal, I-5 corridor).
2. **Civilization:** preservation or loss of knowledge, survival, and infrastructure — the stated purpose of the Atlas playbook.
3. **Institutions:** governance, distribution systems, political rights, psychometric data systems, and perceived covert actors (shadow government, intelligence agencies, platform intermediaries).
4. **Consciousness:** psychological priorities, Maslow-style stages, neurochemistry, metacognition as countermeasure.
5. **Capabilities:** proposed modes of magic/psionic functioning, genetically transmitted memory, thermal/physical abilities.
6. **The Atlas itself:** a network of "data components" claimed to unify the above — with an added interpretive technology (the Mythic Compression Engine) and a temporal substrate (timelines 1–15, time war, quantum lock).

### Central thread

According to REC-002, a population's conditions of life affect the kinds of consciousness its members can access; those states are then connected to alleged capacities. The BATCH-2026-10-08 corpus extends this vertically (relativity engineering as the enabling technology), historically (Moon 1.0/2.0, Ymir/Snowball Earth), and temporally (save-file timelines and the war among them).

This layering is our **interpretation of the internal architecture**, not a claim of scientific proof or a direct author-approved schema.
