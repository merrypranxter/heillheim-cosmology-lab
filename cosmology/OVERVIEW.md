# Cosmology — provisional map

**ATLAS READING • First pass from REC-002**

The Atlas in the available sample spans several nested realms of explanation. This is a *research map*, not a final table of laws adopted by the originating creator.

## The six strata

1. **Events:** military movements and possible conflict in named geographic locations.
2. **Civilization:** preservation or loss of knowledge, survival, and infrastructure.
3. **Institutions:** governance, distribution systems, political rights, and perceived covert actors.
4. **Consciousness:** psychological priorities, relationships, safety and Maslow-style stages.
5. **Capabilities:** proposed modes of magic or psionic functioning.
6. **The Atlas itself:** a network of “data components” claimed to unify the above into an actionable manual.

### Central thread

According to REC-002, a population's conditions of life affect the kinds of consciousness its members can access; those states are then connected to alleged capacities. The same recording places these claims alongside geopolitical forecasts and a purported civilization-preservation program.

This layering is our **interpretation of the internal architecture**, not a claim of scientific proof or a direct author-approved schema.
