## Source priority

1. Original published video/audio when supplied and authenticated;
2. [recording cards](../../sources/) and [atomic claims](../../data/claims.json) (FoxNote-derived / provisional where noted);
3. Perplexity/Gemini secondary synthesis, labelled ATLAS READING;
4. Outside scholarship and primary external documents for outside questions.

These occupy **different evidentiary roles**. One does not override another on attribution. The creator's 75% animal-loss statement remains a faithful source record; an external measurement explaining why the statistic does not mean what it appears to mean belongs **here**, separately.

## Publication policy

A researched analogy is not proof of the whole Atlas mechanism. A real government program is not evidence for an unrelated allegation. Historical myth verses support the words in the text, not arbitrary mappings of those words onto modern geography. No instructions for harmful weapons, infrastructure attacks, unsafe human/animal experiments or restricted-site reconnaissance.

## Reading workflow

For a term: (1) read its source recording and claim; (2) inspect its concordance row; (3) follow its thematic dossier; (4) read the cited primary reference and evidence status; (5) consult the question ledger before resolving ambiguous attributions. See [source-fidelity repair log](../../analysis/source-fidelity-repair-log.md) for prior corrections.
