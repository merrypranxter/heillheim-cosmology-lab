1. **Perplexity's source-ID caveat is now obsolete.** The current repository contains real REC/CLAIM IDs, linked in the master concordance. Some discussed concepts do **not** have atomic claims; label them UNMAPPED instead of inventing attributions.
2. **Avoid unnecessary meta-framing.** Descriptions such as an "information system disguised as mythology" interpret the creator's intent. Describe the actual conceptual system and its information-flow proposals.
3. **The five MCE pillars are in a posted explainer** associated with REC-011; the source provenance and authorship of that explainer are unresolved (Q-019).
4. **Ymir's dates diverge** between REC-011 and REC-038. The Last Glacial Maximum is separated from Cryogenian Snowball Earth by hundreds of millions of years. Neither should replace the other without the original recordings.
5. **The Fenrir/Gleipnir/Cheyenne triangulation** appears in secondary synthesis but has no currently matched atomic claim. Context research is valuable; attribution to the creator is **not cleared**.
6. **Optical-organ detail** similarly needs direct source checking rather than treating prose from Gemini as a verified first-person claim.
7. **Avoid overly strong "contradicted" labels** where a proposed mechanism is merely unsupported, as distinct from a specific false numerical/statistical inference. Apparent absence of evidence is not a demonstrated mathematical impossibility.
8. **WWF freshness update:** Perplexity used the 2024 LPI 73% (1970–2020). WWF released the 2026 report (1970–2022), also 73% average change among monitored vertebrate populations; see EXT-006 and EXT-007. Neither number means 73% or 75% of all animals disappeared.
9. **OrganEx/BrainEx are genuine, important science** but the original papers do not report recovery of conscious personal identity; distinguish post-ischemic cellular restoration from revival of a whole organism.
10. **Bibliography hygiene:** The Perplexity source list repeats archived SEP pages, includes generic search portal links, social posts and off-topic results. Temporary signed S3 links are not durable project sources. Verify a link's contents and date before placing it in a final bibliography.
11. **Official-report distinction:** Government studies being real is not confirmation of all mechanisms attributed to them; AARO's published assessment represents its reviewed evidence rather than a universal proof about every unidentified observation.
12. **Safety and care:** Historical military sites, prospective tactics, clinical revival and technologies described as weapons should remain at documentary conceptual level. Do not generate action plans or self-experimentation guidance.
