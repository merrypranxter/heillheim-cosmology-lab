## C. Key boundaries

A proven source in one discipline validates only its own evidence scope. Eddic mythology → real medieval text, not an independently dated Cryogenian witness. OrganEx → partial cellular restoration, not remembered personal identity. Population index → monitored vertebrate trend, not percentage of all animals. Everett branches → mathematical interpretation, not a working timeline portal. Security history → institutions exist, not proof of one coordinated campaign.

## D. Open data representation

Keep the existing machine-readable graph in data/nodes.json reserved primarily for sourced internal claims. Build a future separate research relation dataset (e.g., external_analogies.json) carrying two endpoints, the external citation ID, relation type, author of interpretation and verification date. This avoids silently turning outside analogies into the creator's own nodes.
