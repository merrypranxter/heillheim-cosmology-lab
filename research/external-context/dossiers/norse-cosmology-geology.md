# Norse accounts, geological epochs and the Moon

**Source anchors:** CLAIM-030-01, CLAIM-038-01/02, CLAIM-011-07, CLAIM-031-01/02/03, CLAIM-034-01, CLAIM-056-01.

## Ancient texts
In the *Prose Edda*'s *Gylfaginning*, Ymir is a primordial being whose body becomes world elements: earth, sea, mountains and sky. Fenrir, the binding with Gleipnir, and Jormungandr occupy different parts of the Norse tradition. The original Eddic motifs are historical literature, not direct descriptions of modern geophysical instrumentation. Start with [Harvard-hosted *Gylfaginning* translation](https://hea-www.harvard.edu/ECT/carl_edda.html) [EXT-005], comparing verses and scholarly editions.

The Gemini synthesis identifies **Fenrir's Gleipnir** with a suggested ecological-key mapping toward Cheyenne Mountain. The current 127 atomic Atlas claim summaries contain **no Fenrir or Gleipnir mention**. The proposed geographic match must therefore remain **secondary research / source-unmatched** until an actual creator segment is recovered. Gleipnir's famously impossible ingredients are meaningful textually; associations with lichen, bird species or springs should be checked as interpretive substitutions, not treated as known historical directions to installations.

## The two Ymir time scales
- **Snowball Earth / Cryogenian:** Neoproterozoic glaciations hundreds of millions of years ago, including the Sturtian interval around 717–660 million years ago in modern geology. See [geochronology lead](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/geological-magazine/article/geochronology-and-formal-stratigraphy-of-the-sturtian-glaciation-in-the-adelaide-superbasin/1D635EDFDB155C19FF8481D178F86AC7).
- **Last Glacial Maximum:** latest major continental-ice maximum tens of thousands of years ago, within prehistoric human time. It is not Snowball Earth.

REC-011's published explainer and REC-038's summary point to different periods. Research both without retroactively reconciling them. An ordinary human oral eyewitness account of an event hundreds of millions of years ago conflicts with established human chronology; that is an outside chronology note, not permission to edit the source statement.

## The Moon and Genesis
REC-031's published explainer describes an earlier Moon's destruction at ~717 million years and Moon 2.0's construction at ~635 million years using an inverse-gravity field. NASA describes the current Moon's origin in the early solar system around **4.5 billion years ago**, with an impact-based formation family central to modern research ([NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/formation/)). These are competing accounts, not corroborating dates.

The Genesis 1 / Chicxulub correspondence occurs in the REC-011 published explainer. The **Chicxulub impact** around 66 million years ago is documented geology; identifying Genesis 1 as a compressed postimpact ecological diary remains an interpretive mapping. Preserve biblical editorial and textual history alongside the geological record.

## Fimbulwinter
REC-056's summary mentions a forecast of a three-year winter. Norse Fimbulwinter has literary traditions of winters without intervening summers. Real analogues include volcanic aerosol winters, impact-induced cooling and food-system effects; these do not date or validate a modern Fimbulwinter prophecy.
