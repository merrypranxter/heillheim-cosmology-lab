# Temporal mechanics: fifteen iterations and the Time War

**Layer:** ATLAS READING — external conceptual context, not a physical confirmation.

## Source anchors and internal architecture
- REC-044 / CLAIM-044-01/02/03: a solar system described as quantum-locked outside normal linear time; the save-game iteration account; fifteen terminal branches.
- REC-039 / CLAIM-039-05/06: information allegedly available from Timeline 14 and current Timeline 15 described as the last iteration.

These claims come from mixed-transcript and summary-only records and have not been original-audio validated.

## External foundations
A computer **checkpoint** stores state so a process can resume or roll back. As an analogy, this is helpful for the source's finite reset mechanism. A machine save state does not establish that the physical solar system has one.

In **general relativity**, some mathematical geometries contain closed timelike curves, but finding a solution to field equations is not demonstrating a buildable or stable time machine. In **quantum foundations**, decoherence explains why alternatives become effectively independent under environmental entanglement. It is not a demonstrated medium for ordinary vehicles or people to travel among histories. Everett-style branching also does not entail *exactly fifteen* worlds. See [SEP on decoherence](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-decoherence/) [EXT-001] and [time travel and modern physics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/time-travel-phys/) [EXT-002].

The **holographic principle** is a technical idea about relations between gravitational theories and descriptions with fewer dimensions. It should not be silently equated with a local projected starfield surrounding an isolated solar system. A Mandela effect is an established term for persistent shared mistaken memories, while the claim of cross-timeline causation is separate.

## What these parallels clarify
- “Iteration” is best documented as an ordered run of a finite process; “branch” as a different continuation; “timelines crossing” is an additional claim, not a consequence of either.
- A “soft spot” may be framed for analysis as a proposed boundary or alignment condition, but frequency matching alone is not spacetime transport evidence.
- Historical UAP accounts can be catalogued by date and sources; age of the report does not independently identify the craft as a vehicle from an alternate iteration.

## Unresolved questions
What initiates a reset? How is state conserved? What defines an iteration's beginning and end? Is Timeline 14 a physical coexisting history or preserved accessible information in his vocabulary? Can a specific predicted divergence be dated and tested? These questions should be answered by source material or creator clarification before creating precise animations, timelines or equations.
