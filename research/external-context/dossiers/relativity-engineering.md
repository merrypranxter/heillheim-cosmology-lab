## Established surrounding fields
**General relativity** models gravitation as spacetime geometry, but no accepted experimental apparatus can shield gravity or switch it by “radiated occlusion.” Electromagnetic levitation, centrifuges, rotation, drop towers and orbiting freefall can reproduce *some engineering effects analogous to changing effective loads*. They are not gravity-field projectors.

In **metallurgy**, directional solidification, selective seeding and controlled heat flow create single-crystal superalloy turbine blades by suppressing grain boundaries. **Microgravity** can reduce buoyancy-driven flow but leaves other transport mechanisms, impurities and defects; it is not automatically perfect crystal growth. See [NASA materials review](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20030056609/downloads/20030056609.pdf) (follow-up source lead).

**Bloch's T³ᐟ² law** describes a low-temperature magnetization relation for specified ferromagnetic systems from thermally excited spin waves. It is not a general heat, gravity or energy-conservation law. Deviations at higher temperatures or different sample geometries do not overturn thermodynamics.

**Time crystals** are experimentally studied nonequilibrium phases with regular order in time; [Zhang et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature21413) and [Choi et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature21426) document discrete time-crystal signatures in specialized driven quantum systems. “Time crystal” does not by itself describe an ingot cast inside a gravity kiln or a guaranteed quantum-computing component.

## Important distinctions
- Material *phase* in physics has multiple technical meanings; a solid phase shift is not evidence that two intact solid volumes can occupy precisely the same space.
- **Energy storage** requires quantitative energy density, reversibility, losses and stability; calling a material a “forever battery” does not supply these measurements.
- Atmospheric or pool waves may be caused by ordinary surface, boundary and interference effects; isolated video is not unique proof of a gravitational wave.
