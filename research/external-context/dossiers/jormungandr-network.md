# Jormungandr, the grid and celestial libraries

**Source anchors:** REC-004 / CLAIM-004-01 through CLAIM-004-04; REC-039 / CLAIM-039-01/03/04; REC-005 / CLAIM-005-04 (summary only).

## Atlas formulation
The Atlas describes underground nodes joined by projected planar relativity fields. The surfaces subdivide Earth into polygon-like territories, with genetically differentiated access to broadcast **celestial libraries**. The source explicitly describes regionally elevated bloodline capacity but also says these lines are meant as **tools for civilization, not rulers**. REC-039 expands the structure into a reciprocal information system in which sensory feedback is used by an AI to model outcomes. Gaia is mentioned separately as another reported knowledge source.

## Real-world conceptual neighbors
- **Graph theory and distributed systems:** nodes, edges, regional routes, redundancy, stored messages and bidirectional feedback are genuine engineering architectures. The correspondences make a proposed system easier to draw; they do not establish its deployment.
- **Spherical tessellation:** geodesic and Goldberg polyhedra, Voronoi territories and clipped polygonal partitions are mathematical ways to subdivide Earth's surface. The soccer-ball analogy in REC-004 is literal source imagery, not a measurement of a planetary device.
- **Ley lines:** a historical landscape-alignment idea associated with Alfred Watkins; later esoteric interpretation changed its meaning. A topographic alignment is not a gravity barrier. See the [Tate history of Watkins](https://www.tate.org.uk/research/tate-papers/06/lines-of-sight-alfred-watkins-photography-and-topography-in-early-twentieth-century-britain) (research lead).
- **Akashic records:** an intellectual tradition of cosmic memory and access, particularly in modern esoteric literature. The Atlas gives that term an asserted technological hardware meaning. See [historical scholarship](https://karolinum.cz/data/clanek/8423/CEJCR_3_2_0109.pdf) (research lead).
- **Genetics and neural interfaces:** DNA can underwrite receptor chemistry and predispositions; epigenetic phenomena may transmit some regulatory effects. That is not evidence of inherited specific technical diagrams, autobiographical experiences or cryptographic keys for an unobserved network.

## Source-versus-analyst boundary
“Biological decryption” is a useful **ATLAS READING** analogy: a body as authentication hardware. If used in a diagram, tag it as an interpretation unless that exact vocabulary appears in verified source audio. Magnetic and electrical sensory detection are real biological phenomena in various animals; they cannot be taken as proof of this proposed human interface.
