## External scholarship
**Ancient Israelite religion** developed over time, with complex textual uses of El, Elohim and Yahweh, regional practice and changing theological claims. Scholarly historical-critical analysis compares layers of language, redaction and historical setting. It does *not* require that a deity's alleged jurisdiction describes the geographic limits of a radio/gravity-grid transmitter.

**Genesis** contains distinguishable literary registers and creation accounts. Comparing Genesis 1's ordering, imagery and vocabulary with Ancient Near Eastern counterparts is legitimate textual research. The **Eden/Tree of Knowledge** theme has long been discussed in terms of divine command, moral agency, knowledge, mortality and human limits. A proposed technical information blockade is an Atlas reading, not a translation compelled by the original Hebrew.

**Anunnaki:** the term names categories of gods in Mesopotamian textual traditions and shifts across periods. The Gemini synopsis gives a direct Anunnaki/Elohim identification; current atomized source descriptions do **not** ground that precise equation securely. Treat as secondary-only pending original citation and specialist Assyriological sources.
