## Documented geography
The Sargasso Sea is an open-ocean region of the North Atlantic subtropical gyre broadly delineated by circulating waters rather than coastlines. Its characteristic floating *Sargassum* ecosystem, currents, eddies and species habitat are real oceanography. Useful institutional leads include the [Sargasso Sea Commission](https://www.sargassoseacommission.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=109) and public [NOAA marine ecology material](https://www.noaa.gov/gc-international-section/marine-protected-areas-mpas-sargasso-sea).

The **Bermuda Triangle** became a persistent maritime legend, but [NOAA](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/bermudatri.html) [additional checked source] reports no evidence that unexplained disappearances occur more frequently there than in other comparably traveled seas. Factors including weather, Gulf Stream variability, shallow hazards and vessel navigation deserve ordinary historical examination. A geometrically appealing spiral is not a statistical disappearance pattern without a specified dataset.
