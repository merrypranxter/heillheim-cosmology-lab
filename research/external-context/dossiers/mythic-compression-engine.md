## The five-pillar account

| Named pillar | Discipline identified in published explainer | Research counterpart | What it doesn't establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware parity | Neuroscience | cognitive archaeology and inference from comparative brain anatomy | that every surviving myth encodes accurate eyewitness science |
| Intentional compression | Information theory | mnemonic narratives, semantic packing, lossy transmission | unique recovery of the original referent |
| Behavioral constants | Evolutionary psychology | recurring human incentives and motive patterns | that a specific text was deliberately corrupted |

| Conceptual lens | Systems ecology | analogy between functions in natural and human systems | identity of two things with a similar structure |

## Intellectual neighbors
Claude Lévi-Strauss's **structural anthropology** asks how relations between mythic units and their transformations across variants create meaning. **Oral-formulaic theory** explains how transmission strategies preserve memorable patterns while changing details. **Geomythology** investigates cases where oral traditions preserve observations of real floods, eruptions, tsunamis or landform changes. It is strongest when checked against independent dates, stratigraphy and archaeological evidence. Starting leads: [Lévi-Strauss manuscript](https://www.freud-lacan.com/wp-content/themes/freudlacan-front/assets/content/2024/02/Article-de-Levi-strauss.pdf), [Māori hazard traditions](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/10/1927/2010/nhess-10-1927-2010.pdf); verify the passages before formal citation.

## Methodological strengthening
Treat each decoding as a **hypothesis** with an explicit mapping algorithm, not a flexible retrospective association. Preserve multiple source translations; name the target event *before* choosing a matching myth; test alternate events and nonmatching texts; require chronological and ecological compatibility; preserve failed matches; measure how often the method makes false positive identifications.

This method can enrich the Atlas without changing the creator's theory. It may also disclose significant useful parallels or contradictions; either outcome belongs in research notes, not as an alteration of the recording.

## Critical open boundary
