# Research protocol

This lab has two parallel kinds of questions.

**Internal cartography** asks: what follows from the Atlas's own rules? Are its categories consistent? Which terms change meaning across recordings?

**External verification** asks: did a described public event happen? Do reputable sources support a physical mechanism? Did a prediction specify a date and falsifiable outcome before the event?

Neither substitutes for the other.

## Evidence log
For each relevant observation collect `source_id`, `timecode`, `claim_id`, `original_words_checked`, `attribution_confidence`, `independent_sources`, and `reviewed_at`.

## Predictions
Distinguish:
- **Forecast:** a time-bound outcome stated before it happened
- **Scenario:** a conditional possible sequence
- **Retrospective interpretation:** later events interpreted as confirmation
- **Alleged personal influence:** the creator claims a direct causal role
- **Symbolic correspondence:** thematic or metaphorical match

Do not upgrade a scenario to a fulfilled forecast without matching date, target, and observable conditions.

## Uncertain transcriptions
Do not silently repair missing pronouns or technical terms. Use `[unclear]` and file a listening question. Timecodes from accelerated user edits are not originals.

## Human context
Analyze gestures descriptively (raised eyebrows, hand movements, glances). Avoid inferring deception, mental health, diagnosis, inner certainty, or threat level from visual behavior alone.
